Pack description
----------------

21 magic sound effects for games and video: spells, sparkles, teleports, portals, vanishing and transformations.

Spells: magic spell cast, ice spell freeze, healing spell, magic shield, dark magic curse, holy light spell, summoning spell.
Sparkles: magic wand sparkle, fairy dust sparkles, twinkle star sparkle, magic shimmer swell, cartoon wink sparkle, sparkle burst.
Teleports: teleport out, teleport in arrival.
Portals: magic portal open, magic portal hum, magic portal close, magic vortex swirl.
Vanish and transformation: magic vanish disappear, magical transformation.

Made in Chisper, a free wavetable synth that runs in the browser (&lt;a href=&quot;https://chisper.qvantor.org&quot; rel=&quot;nofollow&quot;&gt;https://chisper.qvantor.org&lt;/a&gt;): bells, harp, choirs and glassy tones from wavetables, generated noise and inharmonic partials, the synth&#x27;s own chorus, echo and reverb. No recorded samples.
Every sound links to its preset: open it, play the notes and tweak it to render your own version.

Stereo WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, starting right at the sound, loudness-matched (the loudest 100 ms at about -15 LUFS; peaks under -1 dBTP). CC0: free for any use, no credit needed.
