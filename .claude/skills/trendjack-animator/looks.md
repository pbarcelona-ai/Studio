# Looks

Starpop has its own style guides for several of these (`get_skill`: `disney-pixar-animation`,
`cinematic-3d-animation`, `claymation-animation`, `paper-animation`, `wool-crochet-animation`,
`comic-illustration-animation`, `chibi-3d-animation`). Load the matching one when a look is chosen.

Put the chosen `look` line in `ad.json`. It is added to every sheet, still and clip prompt, so describe it once,
in words, and never name a studio, film or franchise. Show the user these options on a card before writing prompts.

| Look                           | `look` line for ad.json                                                                                                                                                                                    | Best for                                                         | Watch out for                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **3D animated film** (default) | 3D animated feature film, stylized CGI in the look of a modern big-studio animated movie: soft subsurface skin, big expressive eyes, appealing rounded shapes, cinematic lighting, highly polished render. | Stories with people, family, emotion                             | Faces drifting: always attach the character sheet                                                               |
| **Glossy 3D product render**   | Clean glossy 3D product visualization, studio lighting with soft reflections, seamless curved backdrop, crisp materials, smooth simple shapes, premium commercial render.                                  | Explainers, cutaways, mechanisms                                 | Too sterile for stories; add a character only if needed                                                         |
| **Claymation**                 | Handmade stop-motion claymation: plasticine characters with visible fingerprints and tool marks, miniature handmade set with felt and wood props, soft practical lighting, shallow depth of field.         | Warm, funny, nostalgic                                           | Smooth motion breaks the illusion: ask for "slightly choppy stop-motion movement" in motion lines               |
| **Watercolor**                 | Hand-painted watercolor illustration on textured cold-press paper: translucent washes, soft pigment blooms, visible paper grain, loose ink linework, warm limited palette.                                 | Gentle, emotional, wellbeing, gifts                              | Fast action smears; keep moves slow and simple                                                                  |
| **Paper cut-out**              | Layered paper-craft diorama: cut cardstock shapes with visible paper fibres, soft drop shadows between layers, handmade miniature world, warm light.                                                       | Storybook, kids and family, simple steps                         | Keep it to 2-4 depth layers; busy scenes turn to clutter                                                        |
| **Wool / felt**                | Cosy handmade knitted and crocheted world: felted wool characters, visible stitches and yarn fibres, soft toy-like set, warm light.                                                                        | Cosy, home, winter, kids                                         | Small details get lost; frame closer                                                                            |
| **Anime / comic**              | Hand-drawn anime-style cel-shaded illustration: clean confident linework, flat colours with soft gradients, expressive faces, speed lines for action, original characters.                                 | Young audiences, energetic hooks                                 | Never copy known characters; keep line weight consistent                                                        |
| **Editorial collage**          | Editorial paper collage: cut-out photographs and textured paper shapes, halftone dots, torn edges, bold limited palette, layered magazine style.                                                           | Explainers, news-style hooks, myths vs facts                     | No invented numbers or charts; keep text out of the images                                                      |
| **Motion graphics**            | Clean flat vector motion graphics: simple geometric shapes, bold limited palette, smooth easing, minimal icons, lots of negative space.                                                                    | Step-by-step, comparisons, how-to                                | Only real numbers; captions carry the words, not the images                                                     |
| **Photoreal phone footage**    | Realistic handheld smartphone video: natural daylight, slight grain, true-to-life skin and materials, shallow depth of field, no studio polish.                                                            | Clones of real creator or founder footage, product-in-hand shots | Faces and hands drift most; attach the real product photo and repeat the hand and setting words in every prompt |

## Motion lines

Every clip prompt opens with the camera, then one action: `Camera: slow push-in from a medium shot to a close-up of
her face. She looks up from the book and smiles.` Useful moves: push-in, pull-back, orbit 30 to 90 degrees, crane down,
top-down tracking, dolly through a doorway, rack focus from hands to face, low dolly toward the product.
Never "static camera".
