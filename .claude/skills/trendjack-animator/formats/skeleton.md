# Format: skeleton demo (spoken or sung, 15-30 s)

Starpop has a full `skeleton-ads` guide: load it with `get_skill` and use it together with this page.

A friendly cartoon skeleton (or an X-ray view of a character) shows what's going on inside: the slumped spine at
the desk, the knees on hard floors, the neck on the wrong pillow. Comic, fast and very visual. Then the product
fixes the picture. Good for posture, footwear, pillows, chairs, bags, anything the body feels.
Audio mode: `"audio": "voice"` (narrator) or `"song"`.

## Beats

1. **Hook**: the skeleton in an everyday pose that's obviously wrong, close, with a comic sound-alike line
   ("This is your spine on a 9-hour workday.").
2. **Exaggerate**: two or three quick shots of it getting worse (comic, never gory).
3. **The swap**: the product arrives (slides in, gets handed over, drops into frame).
4. **The fix**: same pose, now aligned or relaxed, with a glowing highlight where it changed.
5. **Offer**: product hero shot + name + offer.

## Rules

- Keep it light: a cute, rounded cartoon skeleton with big eyes, no realistic bones, blood or pain faces.
- No medical claims. Show comfort and posture in the picture; say only what the product page supports.
- Same skeleton design in every shot: give it a character sheet like any other character (and a prop that
  identifies it, e.g. a striped scarf).
- The X-ray variant: a normal character with a glowing see-through overlay on the body part that matters.
