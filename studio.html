Import

The AI reads the book chosen above and writes its prompts. Nothing is sent to Kaggle or Colab.

If you chose a PDF above that is not uploaded yet, it is uploaded first. Then the PDF is split into chapters, the AI chosen in AI Prompt Writer writes the continuity bible and the shots, narration, sound and music for every chapter, and the CSV files are built. This can take a while and uses that AI. Video and audio are made later, on the Movies tab. The log shows each step.

Cleanup

Removes old generated files. Everything goes to the Trash, so you can put it back.

Keeps the 3 newest notebooks (and the scripts that go with them) and moves the older generated notebooks to the Trash. Also removes Python cache folders, leftover temporary files and empty output folders. Notebooks are re-created automatically when a run needs one. Your books, prompts, characters, clips and images are not touched. You see the list and confirm before anything moves. "Clean up Kaggle notebooks" is separate: it deletes the notebooks this tool pushed to your Kaggle account, except the 3 most recently run. That cannot be undone, so a warning with Continue and Cancel comes first. A notebook that is still running is never deleted.
